SentinelAI
Autonomous Smart Street Intelligence
Building Safer, Greener & Smarter Cities with AI
Executive Summary
SentinelAI is an AI-powered smart street lighting platform leveraging AI, Computer Vision and IoT to automate lighting, improve safety, reduce energy consumption and provide real-time analytics.
Problem Statement
Conventional street lighting wastes energy, relies on manual maintenance, and lacks intelligent monitoring for faults and emergencies.
Solution
The platform dynamically adjusts lighting based on traffic, pedestrians and weather, detects incidents, predicts failures and offers a centralized dashboard.
Core Features
• Adaptive Lighting
• Vehicle & Pedestrian Detection
• Accident Alerts
• Predictive Maintenance
• Energy Analytics
• Smart Dashboard
Technology Stack
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Future Backend: Node.js
Database: Firebase/MongoDB
AI: Computer Vision, Machine Learning
IoT: Motion & Light Sensors
Architecture
Sensors & Cameras → AI Engine → Decision Engine → Smart Lights → Dashboard
Benefits
Lower energy costs, improved public safety, proactive maintenance, and smart city readiness.
Future Roadmap
Mobile app, solar integration, smart traffic integration, emergency response, cloud analytics.

🚦 SentinelAI - Autonomous Smart Street Intelligence

📌 Project Overview

SentinelAI is an AI-powered smart street lighting system that transforms traditional street lights into intelligent safety and energy management systems.

This project uses Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and IoT concepts to improve urban safety, reduce energy consumption, and create smarter cities.

🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional street lights remain ON continuously, causing unnecessary electricity usage. Also, poor lighting conditions can create safety problems such as accidents and suspicious activities during night time.

💡 Solution

SentinelAI provides an intelligent automation system that:

- Automatically controls street lights based on movement detection
- Adjusts brightness according to traffic and environmental conditions
- Monitors energy usage and savings
- Detects accidents and unusual activities
- Sends alerts during emergency situations
